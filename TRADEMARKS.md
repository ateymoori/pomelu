## Forks

You are welcome to fork Pomelu. If you publish a build (in an app store or as
a download), you must:

1. Use a different name and a different icon.
2. Use your own application id instead of `se.royan.pomelu` and your own
   signing keys.
3. Remove or replace `assets/images/`, `assets/characters/` and the store
   metadata.
4. Not suggest that Royan AB made or supports your build.
